## 1. Dashboard & Navigation
Once logged in, the Dashboard provides a high-level view of the business operations. Key indicators typically include:
- **Total Properties:** Number of plots available, reserved, and sold.
- **Recent Leads:** List of latest inquiries.
- **Upcoming Site Visits:** Scheduled bookings for property viewings.
- **Payment Alerts:** Notifications for overdue installments.

Use the **Sidebar Menu** to navigate between modules.

---

## 2. Property Management
This module allows you to manage the inventory of land plots.
- **View Inventory:** List all available properties with details (Size, Price, Location).
- **Add Property:** Enter new plots into the system.
- **Status Updates:** Mark plots as *Available*, *Reserved*, or *Sold*.
- **Location Mapping:** (If applicable) View plots on a map or site plan.

---

## 3. Lead & Customer Management
Manage the relationship with potential and existing buyers.
- **Leads:** Record new inquiries from phone calls, social media, or walk-ins.
- **Site Visits:** Schedule and track property viewings (typically Wednesdays and Saturdays).
- **Customer Profiles:** Maintain records of buyer contact information and purchase history.
- **Follow-ups:** Set reminders to contact potential buyers.

---

## 4. Sales & Payment Tracking
Track the financial aspect of the property transactions.
- **Sales Agreements:** Upload or record signed sales agreements.
- **Installment Management:** Monitor flexible payment plans.
- **Payment Recording:** Log payments made via bank transfer, M-Pesa, or cash.
- **Invoices/Receipts:** Generate and send receipts to customers upon payment.

---

## 5. Document Management (Title Deeds)
A critical part of the Grey Oak workflow.
- **Title Tracking:** Monitor the status of title deed processing (typically 30 days post-full payment).
- **Document Uploads:** Store digital copies of titles and identification documents.
- **Issuance Log:** Record when a title deed has been physically handed over to the client.

---

## 6. Reports & Analytics
Generate reports to analyze business performance.
- **Sales Reports:** View revenue trends over time.
- **Inventory Reports:** See which locations are selling fastest.
- **Outstanding Payments:** Identify clients with pending balances.
