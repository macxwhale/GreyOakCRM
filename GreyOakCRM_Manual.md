## 1. Introduction
The **Grey Oak Real Estate Management System** is a mission-critical platform designed to manage the full lifecycle of land investment. From the first phone call to the final handover of a Title Deed in **Nanyuki, Athi River, or Naivasha**, this system ensures data accuracy, legal compliance, and customer satisfaction.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Access & Security
*   **Portal URL:** `https://greyoakcrm.co.ke/admin/login`
*   **Authentication:** Use your corporate email. If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
*   **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** If enabled, you will receive a code via email to complete your login.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Data Privacy:** This system contains sensitive client information (ID numbers, KRA PINs). Ensure you follow all data protection protocols.

## 3. Lead Management Pipeline
Leads are the lifeblood of Grey Oak. Efficient tracking ensures high conversion rates.

### 3.1 Lead Statuses
*   **New:** Fresh inquiry from social media, website, or walk-in.
*   **Attempted to Contact:** Call made, no pick-up.
*   **Contacted:** Conversation held, needs identified.
*   **Site Visit Scheduled:** Viewing booked (typically Wed/Sat).
*   **Interested:** Attended viewing, choosing a plot.
*   **Lost:** Decided not to purchase.

### 3.2 Lead Conversion
When a lead pays a deposit, click **"Convert to Customer"**. This transfers all history and notes to the permanent Customer module.

## 4. The Sale Journey (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Reserving a Plot
1.  Go to the **Property/Plots** module.
2.  Select the desired plot (e.g., *Nanyuki Phase 2 - Plot 45*).
3.  Change status from **"Available"** to **"Reserved"**.

### Step 2: Sales Agreement & KYC
1.  Create a **Customer Profile**.
2.  Upload **KYC Documents**:
    *   ID Card Copy (Scanned)
    *   KRA PIN Certificate
    *   Passport-sized Photos
3.  Upload the signed **Sale Agreement**.

### Step 3: Invoicing
1.  Go to `Sales > Invoices > Create New`.
2.  Link it to the Customer and the Property.
3.  Set the **Payment Terms** (e.g., 3-month installment plan).

## 5. Financial Management

### 5.1 Recording Payments (Installments)
1.  Navigate to `Sales > Payments`.
2.  Select **"Record Payment"**.
3.  Select the relevant **Invoice**.
4.  Enter the amount and **Payment Mode** (M-Pesa Ref, Bank Slip No, etc.).
5.  Click **"Save and Email Receipt"** to notify the client.

### 5.2 Customer Statements
Clients often request a summary of their payments. 
*   Go to `Customers > [Select Customer] > Statement`.
*   Select the date range and click **"Email as PDF"**.

## 6. Property & Inventory Control
Manage your land bank across various locations (**Kisumu, Diani, Athi River**, etc.).
*   **Inventory List:** View all plots, their sizes (typically 1/8th acre), and current pricing.
*   **Bulk Updates:** Admins can update pricing across an entire project phase simultaneously.

## 7. Document & Title Deed Tracking
The system includes a specialized workflow for the **30-day Title Issuance guarantee**.

1.  **Full Payment Check:** The system flags invoices that are "Paid in Full".
2.  **Documentation Audit:** Verify all legal documents are uploaded.
3.  **Processing Status:** Update the status to **"At Ministry"** or **"Ready for Collection"**.
4.  **Handover Log:** Record the date the client signs for the physical Title Deed.

## 10. Troubleshooting & Support

| Issue | Resolution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Incorrect Balance on Invoice** | Check if all payments (including deposits) have been linked to the invoice number. |
| **Cannot Convert Lead** | Ensure the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields are filled correctly. |
| **Notification Email Not Sent** | Check the "Activity Log" to see if the mail server encountered an error. |
